description: Open OpenPaperDesign Home and its saved Papers.
---

Call the Paper MCP tool `paper_home` with `{"action":"open"}`. Open the returned URL in the host's browser when available; otherwise give the person a clickable link. This is the same Home used by the Codex sidebar. It lists personal Papers and projects previously opened explicitly, without scanning the machine. Do not scaffold another dashboard or save data inside the plugin installation.
