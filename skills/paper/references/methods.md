# Method references

Selected design principles are adapted for the Paper workflow. The installed plugin contains the working instructions; these references are optional background.

- [UI Skills](https://github.com/ibelick/ui-skills/tree/73fb128d046d1f0d74c2909cd26eb616e3415567): deliberate variants, source-owned content and evidence for design guides. [MIT notice](UI-SKILLS-LICENSE.txt).
- [Diorama UI](https://github.com/bydiorama/ui/tree/c3de0b13ef8369056e4fcd586f9d2a76255a7a06): semantic roles, primitive contracts, states and rendered review. [MIT notice](DIORAMA-LICENSE.txt). Its craft references also credit Jakub Krehel’s [MIT-licensed work](CRAFT-LICENSE.txt).
- [Architecture method](https://github.com/openai/plugins/blob/main/plugins/build-web-data-visualization/skills/uml-and-software-architecture-visualization/SKILL.md) and [diagram layout](https://github.com/openai/plugins/blob/main/plugins/build-web-data-visualization/skills/node-link-and-diagram-layout/SKILL.md): audience, abstraction level, semantic edges, label space and layout stability.
- [C4 notation](https://c4model.com/diagrams/notation): explicit responsibilities and meaningful relationships at one abstraction level.

These principles do not require another editor, renderer or service. Source components retain their own contracts and licences. Diagram symbols express common notation subsets rather than formal BPMN/UML validation or format interchange.
